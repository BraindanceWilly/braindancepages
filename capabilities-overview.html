The Five-Part Framework

What makes the Dual Voice System different. A comprehensive toolkit built specifically for cybersecurity founders with a dual audience problem.

North Star

The single sentence that governs everything you say and why you say it. Your north star becomes the filter for every piece of messaging your company will ever create.

Dual Persona & Synthesis

Two contrasting archetypes whose tension produces a voice that is uniquely yours. This duality allows you to speak authentically to both technical and non-technical buyers.

A named human who gives every writer a single editorial test before anything ships. Your Muse ensures consistency across every channel without stifling creativity.
